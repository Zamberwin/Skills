BI Maturity Framework
Assess your organisation's Business Intelligence maturity across 8 dimensions with 40 scored criteria. Each dimension is scored 1–5. Scores generate personalised recommendations.
1
Reactive
Ad hoc reporting. No standards. Data in spreadsheets. BI is a fire-fighting tool.
2
Emerging
Some BI tools in place. Limited governance. Inconsistent KPIs. Siloed efforts.
3
Defined
Standardised platforms. Documented processes. Governed KPIs. Proactive quality.
4
Managed
Measured outcomes. Predictive analytics. Self-service at scale. Data-driven culture.
5
Optimised
AI-driven insights. Continuous improvement. BI as competitive advantage.
🏗 1. Data Foundation & Architecture
Data warehouse design, layer separation (Bronze/Silver/Gold), data modelling, platform modernisation, pipeline reliability, and freshness SLA compliance.
🛡 2. Data Governance & Quality
Ownership, stewardship, DQ rules and scorecards, metadata and lineage, governance council operating model, and remediation process maturity.
📈 3. BI Tools & Reporting
BI platform standardisation, self-service maturity, semantic layer governance, report lifecycle management, and regulatory reporting capability.
🎯 4. KPI & Metrics Management
KPI definition and certification, single source of truth, business alignment and decision support, forecasting integration, and data literacy.
🤖 5. Advanced Analytics & AI
Predictive modelling and ML in production, GenAI and LLM implementation, AI governance and responsible AI, and experimentation framework maturity.
🔐 6. Security & Access Control
Identity and access management, MFA, JIT provisioning, data classification and encryption, audit trail immutability, and cybersecurity posture.
👥 7. Organisation & Culture
Executive sponsorship, team structure and capability, data-driven culture, change management effectiveness, and adoption measurement.
⚙️ 8. Operations & Observability
Pipeline monitoring and alerting, incident management and post-mortems, cost governance and FinOps, and continuous improvement measurement.
