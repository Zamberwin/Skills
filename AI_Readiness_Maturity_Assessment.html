AI Readiness Maturity Framework
This framework assesses your organisation's readiness to adopt, scale, and govern Artificial Intelligence across 9 dimensions and 45 criteria. Each dimension is scored 1–5. The combined score produces a readiness level with targeted improvement recommendations.
1
Unaware
No AI strategy. Minimal data. AI seen as hype. No organisational readiness.
2
Exploring
AI interest sparked. Pilots underway. Skills gaps large. No production AI.
3
Developing
First production AI deployed. Governance emerging. Data foundation improving.
4
Scaling
Multiple AI use cases in production. MLOps in place. AI driving business value.
5
Leading
AI as competitive advantage. Continuous innovation. Responsible AI embedded.
🏗 1. Data Foundation & Quality
AI-ready data pipelines, data quality for ML, feature engineering infrastructure, and the availability of labelled training data across critical domains.
☁️ 2. Technology Infrastructure
Compute (GPU/cloud), storage scalability, ML platform maturity, real-time data serving capability, and ability to deploy and serve models at scale.
👥 3. Talent & Skills
Data science capability, ML engineering skills, domain expertise paired with AI fluency, leadership AI literacy, and talent pipeline maturity.
🎯 4. Strategy & Executive Vision
AI as a strategic priority, executive sponsorship, funded AI roadmap, clear use case prioritisation, and measurable AI value targets.
🛡 5. AI Governance & Risk
AI policy framework, model risk management, regulatory compliance, responsible AI principles, bias and fairness controls, and AI audit capability.
⚙️ 6. MLOps & AI Engineering
Model lifecycle management, CI/CD for ML, feature stores, model monitoring, drift detection, retraining pipelines, and production deployment maturity.
🚀 7. Use Cases & Business Value
Breadth and depth of AI use cases in production, value realisation measurement, ROI tracking, and the maturity of the AI innovation pipeline.
🌱 8. Culture & Change Management
Organisational openness to AI, change management for AI adoption, AI literacy programmes, shadow AI risk management, and data-driven decision culture.
🔐 9. Security, Privacy & Ethics
AI-specific security controls, data privacy in model training, LLM prompt injection defences, adversarial robustness, and ethical AI enforcement.
Assessment
For each criterion, select the level that best describes your organisation's current AI readiness. Be honest — the value is in identifying real gaps.
